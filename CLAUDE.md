@AGENTS.md

Orientation for any task in this repo: ROADMAP.md is the work queue (with the
"⏸ Awaiting user" section for blocked items); ARCHITECTURE.md is the
current-state system map; OPERATIONS.md is the deploy/prod runbook and pending
prod-actions ledger. Every roadmap item gets an ADR created from
docs/adr/TEMPLATE.md before implementation begins (conventions in
docs/adr/README.md).
Every session also records its work (WORKLOG.md entry, ROADMAP row, OPERATIONS
ledger) per the "Record all work" rule in AGENTS.md.
